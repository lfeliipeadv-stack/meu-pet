# Sábio

An old grey wizard with a long white beard, a wide pointed hat and a wooden staff with a curled top.

He walks over your desktop and windows, casts spells with his staff, strokes his beard while thinking, waves, takes a nap on the taskbar and sometimes vanishes in a puff of smoke to reappear somewhere else.

Pixel art made with PixelLab from an original reference sprite.
