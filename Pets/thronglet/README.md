# Thronglet

## Description

A little yellow creature with an antenna on its head that hatches from an egg
and lives on your desktop.

He walks along your taskbar and over your windows, climbs the screen borders,
looks around, naps when left alone and waves at you. He also finds his
adventures: a sword, a shield, a rope, a map to read, a lantern in the night,
a hole to dig, a stone that rolls down the hill...

And from time to time he switches on a jukebox and dances the whole song.

## About pet

Author: Luan Felipe

Original pixel art, drawn from scratch for desktopPet. The sprite sheet holds
416 frames of 64x64 px, arranged in a 16x26 grid.

**Personal pet.** Thronglet is a pet made for this computer and for the
desktopPet gallery. The name and the art belong to the author.

## Animations

* walk, walk 2, turn, drag, toss
* fall (fast, soft and hard landings), sync, idle, look around, sit
* sleep, wake up, wave, hello, laugh, think, point
* sword, shield, rope, read (map), detective (lantern), hike, dig
* sneeze, hot, run, dizzy, shocked, talk, happy, cheer
* hatch (born from the egg), boulder (and boulder gone), kill
* jukebox: appear, wait, on, press, walk, dance, bow, play, off

## Sounds

Six sounds, including the crack of the egg when he hatches, his laugh, the
stone rolling, the jukebox button and the full 32-second music track that
plays while he dances.
