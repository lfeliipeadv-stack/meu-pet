# Pingus

## Description
The penguins of the open source game [Pingus](https://pingus.seul.org/) walking over your desktop.

This pet joins the two Pingus of the gallery in one:

- **Pingus** — the first version, made by **Adriano Petrucci** (2019), with the walk, the fall, the
  balloon, the superman flight and other moves of the game.
- **Pingus Expansion** — a big expansion made by **Zecheriah** (2026) on top of Adriano's pet:
  - 80+ new sprites: tumble and splat after a big fall, angels, sprint, moonwalk, digging through
    windows and screen borders, five rare secret events and more;
  - more interaction: double right-click to explode them (they can also blow each other up with their
    rocket launchers), left-click on the ground to make them jump, throw them around like fireworks;
  - 30+ sound effects;
  - climbing the screen borders and flying around works again.
